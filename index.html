Profile

I work on logic, formal verification and AI. My main research line is spatial model checking for image analysis, started in 2016 and developed in the VoxLogicA tools, from the underlying theory (spatial logics, bisimilarity, minimisation) to high-performance implementations and applications in medical imaging.

My current focus is AI that remains inspectable by people: hybrid AI with a strong symbolic component, and software developed by AI agents under formally grounded supervision.

Open problems I’m working on

1AI agents that produce new results in explainable image segmentation

The problem
Can LLM agents contribute verifiable research progress in symbolic, explainable medical image segmentation?
Why it is hard
Progress must be reproducible and must leave the segmentation procedure readable by a person, not only improve a score.
What I am doing
An ongoing experiment supported by the Anthropic AI for Science Program (2026), built on VoxLogicA 2.

VoxLogicA 2

2A formally verified, high-performance spatial model checker

The problem
Can an image-analysis engine that runs unattended for weeks be both fast and correct by construction?
Why it is hard
Lazy parallel evaluation, caching and GPU kernels are where performance comes from, and also where errors hide; verification and performance engineering usually pull in opposite directions.
What I am doing
VoxLogicA 2.1: its core is a formal model with automatically proved properties, from which efficient implementations are derived.

3Secure, local-first collaboration without central servers

The problem
How can people share and edit data together, including research data, while keeping it encrypted and under their own control?
Why it is hard
It requires user interface, network protocols, cryptography and performance engineering to be designed together; each one constrains the others.
What I am doing
Nearbytes, a friend-to-friend architecture for encrypted, collaborative data spaces (JLAMP 2026).
